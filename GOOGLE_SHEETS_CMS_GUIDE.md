# HeShiv Mobility — Streamlined Google Sheets & Google Drive CMS Guide

This guide details the **simplified, non-technical Google Sheets CMS** for **HeShiv Mobility**.

---

## 🎯 Summary of Unneeded Columns (Removed for Business Owners)

You do **NOT** need to manage technical database IDs, order numbers, or SEO technical flags in your Google Sheet:

- ❌ **`PackageId` / `BlogId` / `GalleryId` / `FAQId` / `TestimonialId`**: Auto-generated by the website code.
- ❌ **`Currency`**: Fixed to **"INR" (₹)** across the entire website.
- ❌ **`DisplayOrder`**: The website displays items in the **exact top-to-bottom order** of the rows in your Google Sheet.
- ❌ **`Active`**: Every row added to the sheet is automatically published (`Active = TRUE`).
- ❌ **`NoIndex` / `CanonicalUrl` / `OgImage`**: Omitted. SEO tags auto-generate from `Title` and `ShortDescription`.
- ❌ **`Slug`**: Auto-generated from `Title` (e.g. *"Somnath Tour"* → `somnath-tour`).

## 📸 Google Drive Image Setup (3 Simple Steps)

1. **Upload:** Upload photos to Google Drive.
2. **Share Access:** Right-click photo → **Share** → set access to **"Anyone with the link" (Viewer)**.
3. **Paste URL:** Copy the link (`https://drive.google.com/file/d/ID/view`) and paste it into the `MainImage`, `FeaturedImage`, or `ImageUrl` column.

> ℹ️ **Image CDN Conversion:** The Angular website automatically converts Google Drive links into high-speed CDN images (`https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d/ID=w1200`) at runtime.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What is `NoIndex` and why was it removed?
`NoIndex` is a technical SEO meta tag (`<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`) that instructs Google **NOT** to index a webpage in search results. Since all your published travel packages and blogs should be indexed by Google, `noIndex` is set to `FALSE` by default in code. Business owners do not need to manage it in Google Sheets.

### What is the use of `PackageId`, `BlogId`, `GalleryId`, `FAQId`, `TestimonialId`?
IDs are used internally by Angular code to efficiently render items on the page (via track keys). The website code automatically generates these IDs from titles (e.g. `pkg-somnath-dwarka-pilgrimage`), so you don't need to type IDs in the sheet.

### How is the order of items controlled?
Items display on the website in the **exact row order** in your Google Sheet (top to bottom). To re-order items on the site, simply re-order the rows in your Google Sheet!
